Family Property Roadmap

Harris County Heirs' Property Navigator — a plain-language tool that helps families understand, organize, and act on tangled-title property issues before they lose the family home.

The ask in one line: Help us turn a working demo into a community-validated, legally reviewed navigator that keeps Harris County families in their homes and protects generational wealth — starting in Kashmere Gardens, built to replicate.

The problem

"Heirs' property" — a home passed down without clear, updated paperwork — is one of the largest, least-visible drivers of household wealth loss in historically underinvested Black and brown communities. Tangled title can block families from repairing, insuring, selling, or refinancing their home; it bars them from disaster-recovery and home-repair assistance; and it leaves them exposed to tax foreclosure and predatory cash-offer investors. Families often don't learn they have a problem until they're at risk of losing the home.

Our solution

A guided, conversational navigator that meets families in plain language and moves them from confusion to clarity. It spots issues, routes families into the right pathway, and generates a personalized Family Property Roadmap: what their situation may mean, which records to gather, which Harris County offices to contact, and what questions to ask a licensed Texas attorney. It is education and navigation — never legal advice, never a score, never a determination of ownership.

Already built (working demo)
Landing, consent, full screener, pathway engine (11 pathways), urgent-issue flags, and a 14-section roadmap with print/save-to-PDF — in English and Spanish. No data leaves the family's device.
What the grant builds
Community co-design, verified resources + attorney legal review, full accessibility, a partner referral & warm-handoff system, an evaluation framework, and a complete Phase 2 pilot proposal.

Why a banking partner

  • CRA & community development: place-based investment in a historically redlined neighborhood.
  • Generational wealth & financial inclusion: clearing tangled title preserves family wealth.
  • Displacement & foreclosure prevention: keeps families housed and properties on the tax roll.
  • Leverage: a small grant seeds a replicable model, not a one-off.
  • Trusted steward: Houston Land Bank's mission and community relationships.
The request: a planning & pilot-readiness grant.
Recommended $150,000 over ~9 months (phase-able from a $75,000 minimum). This funds listening sessions, stakeholder testing, expert content & legal review, full bilingual accessibility, a partner-referral prototype, an evaluation framework, and a fundable Phase 2 pilot proposal.

Educational tool — not legal advice. This program provides general education and navigation support for Harris County residents; it does not create an attorney-client relationship and is not a substitute for a licensed Texas attorney. Program brief

Who we serve

Harris County families navigating heirs' property, probate, title questions, property taxes, exemptions, and housing preservation — beginning with a pilot in Kashmere Gardens / Super Neighborhood 52, and the trusted partners who serve them (legal aid, tax help, probate, home repair, housing counseling, disaster recovery).

How it works (the continuum)

StageWhenGoal
PreventionOwner is livingStop new tangled titles from forming.
InterventionOwner deceased / title unclear / taxes at riskMove families from confusion to organized next steps.
PreservationFamily wants to keep, repair, and protect the homeKeep families housed; protect generational wealth.

What the grant delivers (Phase 1)

  • Community co-design: 4–6 listening sessions and usability testing with 15–25 residents (incl. elders), with participant stipends and trusted-messenger outreach.
  • Content accuracy & legal safety: verify every Harris County office/program; Texas-attorney legal review of all content and disclaimers; complete the 50+ plain-language education cards.
  • Access & equity: finalize the Spanish version, plain-language editing, and an ADA/WCAG accessibility audit (mobile-first, low-data).
  • Partner coordination prototype: consent-based referral routing, warm-handoff tracking, and a navigator dashboard; begin partner MOUs.
  • Measurement: an evaluation framework (aggregate only — no individual scores) and a quarterly reporting dashboard.
  • Replication: a playbook to extend beyond Kashmere Gardens.

Outcomes the funder can hold us to

  • A community-validated, legally reviewed navigator (English + Spanish).
  • A report from 4–6 listening sessions and usability testing.
  • Signed or in-progress partner MOUs.
  • An evaluation framework + baseline metrics, and a full Phase 2 pilot proposal.

Metrics we'll track (aggregate, privacy-protecting)

Families reached and screeners completed · pathway distribution · referrals made and closed-loop completion rate · foreclosure/tax-sale escalations surfaced and routed · time from first contact to referral · denial reasons and bottlenecks · reach in Kashmere Gardens / SN 52 · language needs served.
