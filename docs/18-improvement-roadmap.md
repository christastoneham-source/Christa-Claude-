# Deliverable 18 — Product Improvement Roadmap (for the funding request)

This roadmap shows a funder that the demo is the *front edge* of a credible,
well-scoped build — not a finished product, and not vaporware. Use it to answer the
reviewer's unspoken question: *"What does the money actually buy, and why is this
team the one to do it?"*

## Where the demo stands today (be honest — it's a strength)

**Built and working (Stages 1–2):** landing page, consent gate, plain-language
guided screener, the A–K pathway engine, escalation flags, and a personalized
14-section Family Property Roadmap with print/save-to-PDF. No backend, no data
collection, no scores, no legal conclusions.

**Deliberately not built yet (needs funding + expertise):** verified resource data,
multilingual access, formal legal review, the partner referral/coordination system,
and an evaluation/data layer. Those are exactly what the grant funds.

## Improvement roadmap — what the funding builds

### 1. Community co-design (the heart of the ask)
- 4–6 **listening sessions** in Kashmere Gardens / Super Neighborhood 52.
- **Usability testing** of the demo with 15–25 residents (including elders).
- Participant **stipends** so the people most affected are paid for their expertise.
- Trusted-messenger outreach through churches, libraries, and community navigators.

### 2. Content accuracy & legal safety
- **Verification** of every Harris County office contact, program, and process
  against official sources.
- **Legal review** of all educational content and disclaimers by Texas attorneys /
  legal-aid partners (this protects the org and the families).
- Complete the **full education card library** (50+ plain-language cards).

### 3. Access & equity
- **Spanish version** (and a structure ready for more languages).
- **Plain-language editing** to a verified reading level.
- **Accessibility audit** (ADA/WCAG): screen readers, contrast, mobile-first on
  low-cost phones and limited data.

### 4. Partner coordination system (Stage 3)
- Consent-based **referral routing** and **warm handoff** tracking.
- A **navigator/partner dashboard**: shared screener results, document readiness,
  escalation flags, closed-loop confirmation.
- **Partner MOUs** with legal aid, tax help, probate, repair, and housing partners.

### 5. Measurement & learning (Stage 4)
- An **evaluation framework**: how many families reached, pathways, referrals,
  closed-loop completions, denial reasons, bottlenecks — *aggregate, no individual
  scores*.
- A simple **dashboard** for quarterly reporting to the funder and partners.

### 6. Replication (the funder's upside)
- A **playbook** to extend beyond Kashmere Gardens to other neighborhoods — so the
  funder's dollars seed a *model*, not a one-off.

## Phasing

| Phase | Focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (this grant)** | Co-design, content verification + legal review, Spanish/accessibility, evaluation design, full proposal | A community-validated, legally reviewed demo and a fundable pilot proposal |
| **Phase 2 (pilot)** | Partner referral system, staffed navigation, live pilot in SN 52 | A running pilot with real families and partners, measured |
| **Phase 3 (scale)** | Replication to additional neighborhoods | A countywide model |

## Why this team, without an external consultant

- The strategy, logic, content, and a working demo already exist (this repository).
- Phase 1 is **operational support + community engagement + expert review** — not a
  from-scratch software contract. The org leads it; specialized expertise (legal
  review, evaluation, translation) is brought in as scoped line items, not a general
  consultant retainer.

> *Framing note for the proposal:* lead with the families and the demo, then the
> roadmap. Funders fund momentum + a clear plan. You have both.
