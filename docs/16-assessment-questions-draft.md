# Deliverable 16 — First Draft of the Assessment Questions

Draft, plain-language wording for the guided screener. Pair with the routing logic
in [Deliverable 4](./04-assessment-question-flow.md). Unless noted, every question
shows these answer options:

> **Yes · No · I do not know · I need help finding this · This does not apply · I
> want to skip this**

Microcopy convention: *italic* lines are gentle helper text shown under the question.

## Section 1 — Welcome & Consent

> Welcome. This tool helps Harris County families understand what may be going on
> with a family home or property, what records to gather, which offices or partners
> may help, and what questions to ask a licensed Texas attorney.
>
> **A few important things:**
> - This is **educational only**. It is **not legal advice** and does not create an
>   attorney-client relationship.
> - It will **not** decide who owns the property or who the heirs are, and it will
>   not tell you to file, sign, sell, or transfer anything.
> - For advice about your situation, please speak with a **licensed Texas attorney**.
> - **Please do not enter** Social Security numbers, full bank account numbers, or
>   other highly sensitive information.
> - We only share your information with a partner **if you give permission**.
>
> ☐ **I understand this tool is educational and does not provide legal advice.**

## Section 2 — Property Location

1. **Is the property in Harris County, Texas?**
   *If it's in another county, we can still share general information, but the
   offices and records there may be different.*
2. **What is the property address?** *(Street, city, ZIP. You can skip if you're not
   sure.)*
3. **Is the property inside the City of Houston?**
4. **Is the property in Kashmere Gardens or Super Neighborhood 52?**
   *This helps us connect you with the closest local resources.*
5. **Right now, is the property…** *(choose one)* occupied · vacant · rented ·
   abandoned · damaged · I'm not sure.
6. **What kind of property is it?** *(choose one)* a home · a vacant lot ·
   multifamily (like a duplex/apartment) · commercial · church-owned · other.

## Section 3 — Current Owner Status

1. **Who is listed as the owner, if you know?** *(A name is fine. Skip if unsure.)*
2. **Is that person living or deceased?** *(living · deceased · I do not know)*
3. **Are you the listed owner?**
4. **Are you related to the listed owner?**
5. **Do you live in the property?**
6. **Is the property listed as "in the estate of" someone?**
   *You might see this on a tax statement or HCAD record.*
7. **Do you know whether the deed has been updated since the owner's situation
   changed?**

---

## Section 4 — If the Owner Is Living  *(Prevention)*

> Good news — because the owner is living, this is a chance to plan ahead and
> prevent future problems.

1. **Does the owner have a will?**
2. **Does the owner have a trust?**
3. **Has the owner named who should receive the property?**
4. **Has the owner thought about a transfer on death deed or another planning tool?**
   *A transfer on death deed can name who gets the property automatically — an
   attorney can explain if it fits.*
5. **Does the owner have a homestead exemption?**
6. **Does the owner have an over-65, disability, veteran, or other exemption?**
7. **Does the family know where the deed and property records are kept?**
8. **Are family members aware of the owner's wishes?**

---

## Section 5 — If the Owner Is Deceased

> We're sorry for your loss. These questions help us understand what records may
> exist. "I don't know" is completely okay.

1. **About when did the owner pass away?** *(A year is fine.)*
2. **Was there a will?**
3. **Is the original will available?**
4. **Was the will filed with a court?**
5. **Was probate opened?** *Probate is the court process for handling someone's
   estate.*
6. **Was anyone named executor or administrator?**
7. **Was there a trust?**
8. **Was there a transfer on death deed?**
9. **Was there an affidavit of heirship?** *A sworn statement about family history
   sometimes used for property.*
10. **Was there a small estate affidavit?**
11. **Were any court orders issued?**
12. **Is there disagreement among family members?**
13. **Are there multiple people who might be heirs?**
14. **Are the property taxes current or delinquent?** *(current · delinquent · I do
    not know)*

## Section 6 — Family & Heirship Context

> We can't decide who the heirs are — only an attorney or court can. These questions
> just help us point you to the right questions and records.

1. **Was the deceased owner married?**
2. **Did the deceased owner have children?**
3. **Are any of those children also deceased?**
4. **Are there grandchildren, siblings, parents, or other relatives who may be
   involved?**
5. **Are you one of several possible heirs?**
6. **Does anyone disagree about who should receive the property?**
7. **Has anyone been paying the taxes, insurance, repairs, or utilities?**
8. **Has anyone tried to sell, transfer, refinance, lease, or repair the property?**

## Section 8 — Real Property Records

1. **Do you have the deed?**
2. **Do you know whose name is on the deed?**
3. **Has the property ever been transferred to someone?**
4. **Has anyone searched Harris County real property records for this property?**
5. **Are there any liens, deeds of trust, releases, judgments, or tax liens on it?**
   *These are recorded documents that can affect the property.*
6. **Has a title company ever reviewed the property?**

## Section 9 — Taxes & Exemptions

> Reminder: exemptions may lower taxes, but they don't by themselves clear up who
> owns the property.

1. **Are the property taxes current?**
2. **Are the taxes delinquent (behind)?**
3. **Has the family received notices from the tax office, a court, an attorney, or a
   collection firm?**
4. **Is there a tax lawsuit?**
5. **Is there a foreclosure or tax-sale notice?** *If yes, this may need urgent
   attention — we'll help you prepare.*
6. **Is there a payment plan in place?**
7. **Is there a homestead exemption?**
8. **Is there an over-65 exemption?**
9. **Is there a disability exemption?**
10. **Is there a disabled veteran exemption?**
11. **Does the person living there use it as their main home?**
12. **Does that person's Texas ID or driver's license match the property address?**

## Section 10 — Property Condition & Housing Stability

1. **Is the home safe to live in?**
2. **Are repairs needed?**
3. **Has the family been denied home-repair help because of the title?**
4. **Has the family been denied disaster-recovery help because of the title?**
5. **Is there insurance on the property?**
6. **Are the utilities active?**
7. **Are there code violations?**
8. **Is there illegal dumping, trespassing, fire damage, or storm damage?**
9. **Is anyone at risk of losing their place to live (displacement)?**
   *If someone elderly or disabled is at risk, please tell us — we'll prioritize
   support resources.*

## Pathway-specific attorney question banks (used in the roadmap)

These pre-written questions populate Roadmap §8 based on triggered pathways.

**A — Living Owner Prevention:** What estate planning documents fit this property?
Would a will, trust, transfer on death deed, or other tool help? How can the owner
protect the property while living? What happens if the owner dies without a will?
How should wishes be documented when several family members are involved?

**B — Deceased With Will:** Does this will need to be probated? Is the original
required? Who has authority to act for the estate? Does the will clearly identify the
property? What if the will was never filed? What records are needed before
transferring, repairing, selling, or refinancing?

**C — Deceased Without Known Will:** What happens when someone dies without a will in
Texas? Who may have an interest? Is probate needed? Is an affidavit of heirship
appropriate? Is a small estate affidavit possible? What records clarify title? Can
one heir act alone, or must all participate?

**D — Passed Down (informal):** Was the property legally transferred? Does family
understanding match the public record? What documents clarify ownership? Can the
person who received it sell, repair, refinance, or apply for help? What if other
heirs disagree?

**E — Multiple Heirs:** Who are the possible heirs? Must each agree before action?
What if one wants to sell and another to keep it? Can one person represent the
estate? What documents show authority? What are the risks of signing without all
heirs?

**F — Family Conflict/Pressure:** What rights may each family member have? What if
heirs disagree about selling or keeping? What if someone is pressuring another to
sign? Can the family make an agreement? When is mediation helpful? What legal options
exist if the family can't agree?

**G — Tax/Foreclosure Risk:** Is the property in tax foreclosure? Has a lawsuit been
filed? Is there a deadline to respond? Can a payment plan, deferral, exemption, or
legal defense apply? What if multiple heirs are involved? Can delinquent taxes block
transfer, repair help, or sale?

**H — Keep & Repair:** What proof of ownership is needed for repair assistance? Can
heirs apply if the deed is still in a deceased person's name? What title documents
are needed? Can the occupant apply for a homestead exemption? What steps protect the
home going forward?

**I — Sell/Transfer/Redevelop:** Who has authority to sell or transfer? Are all heirs
required to sign? Are there title defects? Are taxes, liens, or probate issues
blocking a sale? What if one heir refuses to sign? What should be reviewed before a
purchase contract?

**J — No One Knows Where to Start:** What documents should I gather first? How do I
find out who legally owns it? What if the deed is still in my deceased relative's
name? What if I can't find a will? What if I don't know whether probate happened?
What are the first steps before making decisions?

**K — Vacant/"In the Estate Of":** Who has authority to act for this property? Is it
in an estate? Are there known heirs? Are there delinquent taxes, liens, or code
issues? Could the Public Probate Administrator assist? What public agency or housing
options may be available?
