# Deliverable 2 — Resident User Journey

The resident journey moves a family **from confusion to clarity** while keeping
the experience warm, unhurried, and free of judgment. Many users arrive carrying
grief, family conflict, mistrust, tax stress, or fear of losing a family home.
The journey never feels like a test and never reduces a family's situation to a
number.

## Journey at a glance

```
Arrive → Understand → Consent → Describe → Get Roadmap → Ask & Prepare → Get Help
```

## Stage 1 — Arrive (Landing)

**Mindset:** "Someone said my house has a title problem and I don't know what
that means."

- Reads the core promise and a one-paragraph explanation of heirs' property.
- Sees plainly what the tool **does** and **does not** do.
- Sees the educational disclaimer up front.
- Chooses: *Start your roadmap*, *Learn first*, or *Browse resources*.

**Success:** the resident feels this is for people like them, in their county,
in plain language.

## Stage 2 — Understand (Education-first, optional)

**Mindset:** "Before I answer questions, what is this even about?"

- Browses education cards (heirs' property, probate, deed, exemptions…).
- Views the document checklist and Harris County office guide.
- Can start the assessment at any time.

## Stage 3 — Consent (Welcome & Consent)

**Mindset:** "Is this safe? Who sees my answers?"

- Learns: educational only; not legal advice; no attorney-client relationship;
  may need a licensed Texas attorney.
- Is told **not** to enter SSNs, full bank account numbers, or highly sensitive
  information.
- Learns information is shared with partners **only with consent**.
- Confirms: *"I understand this tool is educational and does not provide legal
  advice."*

**Success:** the resident trusts the tool enough to continue.

## Stage 4 — Describe (Guided Screener)

**Mindset:** "I don't know all the answers and I'm afraid that's a problem."

- Answers **one question at a time**, in plain language.
- Every question allows: *Yes · No · I do not know · I need help finding this ·
  This does not apply · I want to skip this.*
- Never made to feel embarrassed for not knowing.
- Sees gentle inline education ("What's a deed?") on demand.
- Can pause and resume; can go back and change answers.
- If an **escalation flag** triggers (tax sale, court papers, pressure to sign),
  sees supportive urgency language immediately — not alarm, not a score.

**Emotional design:** progress shown as a calm journey ("You're almost there"),
never a grade. Sensitive sections (family conflict, displacement) are framed with
care and an easy skip.

## Stage 5 — Get Roadmap (Family Property Roadmap)

**Mindset:** "Okay — so what do I actually do now?"

- Receives a personalized **Family Property Roadmap** (no scores, no legal
  conclusions) including:
  - What you shared (plain-language summary)
  - What this may mean (issue spotting)
  - Recommended pathway(s) and why
  - Records to gather
  - Harris County offices to contact and why
  - Questions to ask an attorney
  - Questions to ask public offices
  - Non-legal support referrals
  - Things to avoid until legal review
  - Suggested next 30 days
  - Longer-term protection steps
- Can **download PDF** or **email it to myself**.

**Success:** the resident leaves with a concrete, ordered set of next steps.

## Stage 6 — Ask & Prepare (Conversational Navigation Mode)

**Mindset:** "Can I ask one more thing?"

- Asks follow-up questions and receives education, issue-spotting, records lists,
  office referrals, and attorney questions.
- If they ask for legal advice, hears: *"I cannot provide legal advice or tell you
  what legal action to take. I can explain the general issue, help you identify
  records to gather, and suggest questions to ask a licensed Texas attorney,"* then
  is helped educationally.

## Stage 7 — Get Help (Consent-based referral)

**Mindset:** "I'm ready to talk to a real person."

- Reviews exactly **what** would be shared, **with whom**, and **why**.
- Consents (or declines) to a warm handoff to legal aid, tax, repair, housing, or
  foreclosure-prevention partners.
- Receives confirmation and what to expect next.

## Emotional throughline

| Resident feeling on arrival | What the journey provides |
|---|---|
| Overwhelmed | One question at a time; calm pacing |
| Ashamed of not knowing | "I do not know" is always a valid answer |
| Distrustful | Clear consent, plain disclaimers, no data sold |
| Afraid of losing the home | Preservation pathways and urgent escalation help |
| Stuck / no starting point | "No One Knows Where to Start" pathway + 30-day plan |

## Accessibility & inclusion commitments

- Plain language (target ~6th–8th grade reading level); jargon explained inline.
- English and Spanish at launch; structure ready for additional languages.
- Mobile-first; works on a phone with limited data.
- Screen-reader friendly; large tap targets; high contrast.
- Designed to be delivered through trusted community touchpoints (churches,
  libraries, community navigators) as well as self-service.
