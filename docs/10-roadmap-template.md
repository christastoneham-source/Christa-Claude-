# Deliverable 10 — Family Property Roadmap Template

The roadmap is the platform's primary output. **No scores. No risk ratings. No
legal conclusions.** It is structured, plain-language, and customized by the user's
answers, triggered pathways, and stated goal. A fully worked sample appears in
[Deliverable 17](./17-roadmap-output-draft.md).

**Title:** *Family Property Roadmap: Harris County Heirs' Property Navigator*

## Section structure (14 sections)

### 1. Educational Disclaimer
Core disclaimer + framing statement (from [Deliverable 8](./08-legal-disclaimer-language.md)).
States clearly: not legal advice; no attorney-client relationship.

### 2. What You Shared
Plain-language summary of the user's answers, including:
property location · occupancy status · current owner status · owner living or
deceased · whether there is a known will · whether probate is known · whether there
may be multiple heirs · tax status · exemption status · user's goal.
*(Only what the user provided; "I do not know" is reflected honestly.)*

### 3. What This May Mean
Issue-spotting observations only — no conclusions. Template sentence:
> "Because [fact from answers], this may be a situation where [issue] needs further
> review. This is not a legal conclusion."

### 4. Recommended Pathway or Pathways
Lists the one or more triggered pathways (A–K) by name, ordered per the decision
tree's priority logic ([Deliverable 5](./05-pathway-decision-tree.md)).

### 5. Why This Pathway Was Selected
Plain-language reason tied to specific answers (e.g., "You shared that the listed
owner has passed away and the family is not sure whether there was a will.").

### 6. Records to Gather
Customized checklist drawn from: deed · death certificate · will · trust · probate
case records · court orders · affidavit of heirship · small estate affidavit ·
transfer on death deed · tax statement · HCAD property account · homestead/over-65/
disability/veteran exemption records · liens or releases · utility/insurance/repair/
occupancy records · family tree · heir contact list.
Each item notes **where it may be found / who may know**.

### 7. Harris County Offices to Contact
Relevant offices with **why each may matter**: Harris County Clerk · Probate Courts
· Public Probate Administrator · HCAD · Tax Assessor-Collector · legal aid/attorney
resources · Houston Land Bank (if relevant). Pulled from
[Deliverable 7](./07-resource-directory.md).

### 8. Questions to Ask an Attorney
Customized to the triggered pathway(s). (Pathway-specific question banks are defined
in [Deliverable 16](./16-assessment-questions-draft.md) and the master pathway
descriptions.)

### 9. Questions to Ask Public Offices
Customized questions for: Harris County Clerk · Probate Courts · Public Probate
Administrator · HCAD · Tax Assessor-Collector.

### 10. Non-Legal Support Referrals
Relevant categories: tax exemption support · foreclosure prevention · home repair
pre-eligibility · housing counseling · disaster recovery assistance · community
navigation · Houston Land Bank referral (if relevant) · Public Probate Administrator
referral (if relevant).

### 11. Things to Avoid Until Legal Review
Pathway-specific cautions, e.g.: don't sign a deed or sale contract under pressure ·
don't assume one family member can act for everyone · don't ignore tax notices,
court notices, or certified mail · don't rely only on family memory if public
records differ · don't spend major money on repairs before understanding ownership
and tax status · don't accept cash offers without reviewing title, taxes, and
authority.

### 12. Suggested Next 30 Days
Practical, non-legal steps, e.g.: confirm the property address · look up the HCAD
property account · search for the deed · ask family about wills/trusts/probate/court
action · confirm whether taxes are current · gather documents in one folder ·
contact legal aid or a licensed Texas attorney if ownership is unclear · contact the
right public office with prepared questions.

### 13. Longer-Term Protection Steps
Estate planning for living owners · annual exemption review · document organization
· family property meeting · legal review before signing · home repair & preservation
planning · generational wealth protection planning.

### 14. Closing Message  *(verbatim)*
> "You do not have to resolve every issue at once. The first step is understanding
> what is known, what is missing, and who can help. This roadmap is designed to help
> you prepare for informed conversations with qualified professionals and public
> agencies."

## Generation rules

- Sections are **always present** in this order; content within them is
  conditionally generated from answers and triggered pathways.
- If an escalation flag is active, Section 3 and Section 4 lead with the urgent
  pathway and the escalation language ([Deliverable 11](./11-escalation-flag-logic.md)).
- All generated text passes the **do-not-say** / anti-conclusion filter
  ([Deliverable 8](./08-legal-disclaimer-language.md)).
- Output formats: on-screen, downloadable PDF, and email-to-self. Disclaimer appears
  on first and last page of exports.
- Honor "I do not know" honestly — convert unknowns into records-to-gather and
  office-question items rather than guessing.
