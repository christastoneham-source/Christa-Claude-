# Review Workflow — 7811 Harrisburg Development Partner RFP

This is the step-by-step method Claude follows for every submission, applied
**identically** to each one so the process is fair and documented.

## Roles

- **Reviewers (up to 3):** each independently scores every submission using the
  official 100-point rubric. Independent scoring before discussion is good
  committee practice and produces a stronger record.
- **Claude:** plain-language analyst. Translates finance/LIHTC concepts, checks
  responsiveness, drafts each reviewer's structured report, flags missing
  information and red flags, and prepares the cross-submission comparison.
  Claude does **not** decide the award and does **not** give legal advice or a
  final underwriting opinion.

## Step-by-step, per submission

1. **Confirm scope.** List the files received for the submission; note anything
   that appears missing. Proceed with a *provisional review* rather than stalling.
2. **Provisional read.** Quick plain-language snapshot + bottom line so reviewers
   can react early.
3. **Responsiveness gate.** Run the 33-item checklist
   (`templates/responsiveness-checklist.md`). Mark Provided / Partially /
   Missing / Unclear, with notes and risk level. Flag possible non-responsiveness.
4. **RFP alignment.** Rate each project requirement: Strongly / Generally /
   Partially / Weakly aligned / Not addressed.
5. **Financial feasibility review.** Sources & uses, cost per unit, gap,
   debt/equity/subsidy, NOI/DSCR if rental, commitment status, whether
   affordability matches the assumed rents and debt, who carries risk, and
   whether HLB is being asked to absorb risk directly or indirectly.
6. **LIHTC / capital stack review.** If credits/bonds are referenced: 4% vs 9%,
   volume cap, schedule vs application cycles, syndicator/investor, equity
   pricing, bridge/construction/permanent conversion, soft debt/gap, compliance
   experience. If unclear, treat as a *clarification* issue, not an automatic
   penalty.
7. **Community benefit & design review.** Response to prior engagement,
   neighborhood fit (single-family character, no townhome typologies), green
   space + maintenance/security, community-serving space, MWBE/HUB, local hiring,
   resident services, Complete Communities, anti-displacement.
8. **Score the rubric.** Provisional score per category with evidence, strengths,
   concerns, missing info, clarifying questions, and confidence (High/Med/Low).
   Reward evidence; penalize vague/speculative claims; don't over-penalize
   creative proposals where the gap is clarifiable.
9. **Red flags.** Compliance, finance, LIHTC, design/site, community benefit,
   operational, legal/deal-structure, schedule, and HLB risk exposure.
10. **Questions for the reviewer.** A few targeted questions to sharpen scoring.
11. **Questions for the developer.** Grouped clarification questions for a
    written interview (finance, LIHTC, ground lease, affordability, design,
    community, operations, schedule, legal, HLB risk protection).
12. **Recommended next steps.** Advance to interview / advance with conditions /
    hold for clarification / do not advance / unable to determine — with
    rationale, conditions, top three issues, and suggested internal handoffs
    (legal, finance, community engagement, design).

## Scoring discipline

- Scores are **provisional** until the reviewer confirms final scoring.
- Always separate a **scoring issue** (a genuine weakness) from a
  **clarification issue** (something merely missing from the paperwork that the
  two-stage written interview can resolve).
- Use only evidence from the submission; reference the section/attachment.
  Never invent missing facts — flag them.

## Output and filing

- Each reviewer's full report per submission is saved as
  `reviews/reviewer-N/<submission>-report.md` (from the report template).
- Each reviewer's category scores feed the aggregated scorecard
  (`comparison/aggregated-scorecard.md`).
- After all submissions are reviewed one by one, the side-by-side comparison is
  saved as `comparison/comparison-analysis.md`.

## Naming convention

- `submission-A`, `submission-B`, `submission-C` map to developer/team names
  recorded in `comparison/submission-index.md` (create when names are known).
- Keep the same submission letters across all reviewers so the record lines up.
