### 1. Proposal Snapshot

unit mix by bedroom; affordability mix by AMI; community-serving space;
ground lease vs purchase; total development cost; requested HLB
contribution/concession; financing sources; timeline; key partners; major
claimed community benefits.

### 2. Bottom Line
Strongest feature; biggest concern; whether the proposal appears competitive.
*"This proposal appears strongest in ___, but the main issue for reviewers to
examine is ___."*

### 3. Responsiveness Review
Attach/complete the 33-item responsiveness checklist. Summarize any
possible non-responsiveness here.

### 4. Alignment with RFP Requirements
Plain-language rating per requirement (Strongly / Generally / Partially /
Weakly aligned / Not addressed): ≥25 units; ≥51% residential; all ≤120% AMI;
deeper affordability; ground-floor community space; multigenerational design;
universal accessibility; sustainability; single-family character / no townhome
typology; no Harrisburg curb cuts; safe access; green space; maintenance +
security; sound/traffic mitigation; TOD; community-serving retail; lead developer.

### 5. Financial Feasibility and Capital Stack Review
Sources & uses; total cost and cost/unit; hard/soft/land/financing costs;
developer fee; contingency; operating expenses and reserves; revenue; NOI; debt
service; DSCR if provided; permanent loan sizing; gap; commitment status of each
source; whether affordability matches assumed rents/debt; whether community
space is funded and sustainable; who carries predevelopment/construction/
lease-up/operating risk; whether HLB is asked to absorb risk; ground lease vs
purchase impact on HLB and affordability. *Explain concerns in plain language.*

### 6. LIHTC and Affordable Housing Finance Considerations
Is the LIHTC pathway clear (4% + bonds vs 9% vs other)? Volume cap? Schedule vs
application/award cycles? Syndicator/investor/lender named? Equity committed vs
estimated vs speculative? Credit pricing assumptions? Bridge/construction/
permanent conversion and soft debt? Does affordability match the structure?
Prior LIHTC closing and compliance experience? Treat unclear LIHTC as a
*clarification* issue.

### 7. Community Benefit and Design Review
Response to prior engagement; neighborhood fit; multigenerational support;
single-family character / townhome concern; green space + maintenance/security;
community-serving space and local business support; MWBE/HUB; local hiring/
training; resident services; Complete Communities; anti-displacement; long-term
community value.
