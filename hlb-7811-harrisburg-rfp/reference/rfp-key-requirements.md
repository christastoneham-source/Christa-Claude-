## Project requirements (Section 3.0)

- At least **25 total housing units** (rental and/or for-sale).
- At least **51% of the site** designated for residential use.
- **All units affordable at or below 120% AMI.** Deeper affordability
  (below 80% AMI, and units at/below 50% and 30% AMI) **prioritized**.
- **Ground-floor community-serving space**, preferably along Harrisburg frontage.
- Design supports **multigenerational households**, universal accessibility,
  and sustainable building practices.
- Reflects **single-family character** of surrounding blocks, especially the
  north side. Community **opposed townhome-style typologies**.
- **No direct curb cuts** along Harrisburg; safe pedestrian/vehicular access.
- Green/park space and shared public amenities, with maintenance + security.
- Transit-oriented design; sound/traffic mitigation along Harrisburg.
- If multiple entities, identify a **lead/master developer** and primary contact.

## Deal structure (Section 4.0)

- HLB retains fee simple; site sold or ground leased.
- Under a lease: HLB takes a significant portion as **capitalized payments at
  construction commencement and completion**, then **annual payments based on a
  negotiated % of NOI**.
- **Permanent affordability covenant** recorded before construction.
- Development Partner (or affiliate) **creates the ownership entity** and serves
  as **guarantor**. **HLB provides no financing guarantees.**
- Developer secures **all** predevelopment, construction, and permanent financing.
- Ongoing community process: community meetings in pre-development (≥1 not less
  than two weeks before any major funding application); copies of all funding
  applications to HLB; semi-annual reporting during construction and first 3–5
  years, then annual. **Final design approval rests with HLB.**

## Minimum qualifications (Section 5.2)

- Legally incorporated, able to contract in Texas and with HLB.
- All IRS tax returns filed.
- **Most recent certified financial audit or compiled financials (within 2 years).**
- Insurance meeting HLB standards; able to name HLB as insured.
- Comply with Federal Funding Source Requirements, Harris County Minimum
  Property Standards, and Tenant Protection Policy.
- Pay HLB title insurance and third-party transaction costs.
- Provide all required guarantees.
- Disclose any real/apparent conflict of interest.

## Required technical proposal contents (Section 6.0)

Proposals missing required information **may be deemed non-responsive.**

- **6.1 Cover Letter / Executive Summary** — ≤2 pages, signed by authorized
  representative; identifies team + primary contact; summarizes units,
  affordability mix, community uses; **explicitly** acknowledges Exclusive
  Negotiation Period, commits to permanent affordability covenant and deal terms,
  and confirms respondent bears all proposal costs.
- **6.2 Development Team Qualifications** — name/address/contact/email for
  Developer/Co-Developer, Architect, Engineer, General Contractor, Property
  Manager, and key consultants (e.g., equity provider/syndicator); resumes +
  relevant experience; identify MWBE/HUB partners; confirm legal ability to
  contract in TX and with federal funding sources.
- **6.3 Prior Development Success** — at least **two** comparable projects with
  location, size, unit mix, affordability, funding sources/strategy, partnership
  structure, community engagement, current occupancy, operating performance,
  lessons learned; **attach approved underwriting pro forma / lender- or
  investor-approved financials** for each.
- **6.4 Financing Strategy & Funding Gap** — detailed **sources-and-uses** with
  commitment status (soft/hard/pending); preliminary pro forma covering debt
  service and community-serving space; gap-closing strategy; copies of funding
  commitments; **certified audit (within 2 years) for the guarantor entity**;
  willingness to provide all guarantees.
- **6.5 Project Concept & Design** — narrative + conceptual drawings (site plan,
  massing, unit mix, ground-floor/community space, parking); single-family
  character; multigenerational; universal accessibility; sustainability;
  sound/traffic mitigation; green/public space; **AMI table by band (30%,
  31–50%, 51–65%, 66–80%, 81–120%) and by bedroom count.**
- **6.6 Community Engagement & Neighborhood Impact** — engagement plan
  (min. meetings + outreach), local business/nonprofit/service partnerships,
  Complete Communities alignment, community benefits (local hiring, MWBE).
- **6.7 Staffing & Project Organization** — org chart, key personnel roles +
  location; list up to **four** members (with emails) for written interview.
- **6.8 Work Plan & Development Schedule** — selection → closing → construction →
  stabilization; key financing application dates (LIHTC/bond volume cap); design
  reviews; community meetings; critical path; risk mitigation.
- **6.9 Cost Proposal** — itemized budget; requested HLB participation;
  purchase/ground-lease assumptions; equity; operating reserves; confirm
  compliance with **cost-reasonableness standards of 2 CFR 200.404**.
- **6.10 References** — at least **three** (name, title, email, phone).

## Official 100-point scoring rubric (Section 7.2)

| # | Category | Points | Evaluation focus |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Development Team Qualifications & References | 15 | Experience/capacity in affordable, mixed-income, LIHTC; property management strength; MWBE/HUB; ≥3 references confirming similar delivery. |
| 2 | Financing Strategy & Cost Competitiveness | 20 | Strength/completeness of sources-and-uses and pro forma; commitment status; financial capacity/readiness; reasonable, competitive total cost and land terms; ability to close the gap. |
| 3 | Project Concept, Design & Program | 20 | Site plan/design quality; sustainability; universal accessibility; neighborhood fit; community meeting space + ground-floor retail where feasible. |
| 4 | Affordability & Housing Mix | 20 | Depth of affordability (≤50% AMI, ≤30% where possible); covenant clarity; strength of unit-size and tenure mix (rental + for-sale). |
| 5 | Community Engagement & Local Benefits | 15 | Defined engagement plan; locally owned/underrepresented business partnerships; local employment/training; resident services; Complete Communities alignment. |
| 6 | Implementation Readiness & Schedule | 10 | Realistic, detailed schedule; critical-path items; risk mitigation. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Process notes (Sections 7.3–7.4, 10.0)

- **Two-stage review:** (1) responsiveness check + scoring; (2) optional written
  interview/presentation for top-scoring teams; responses inform final scoring.
- Committee may waive **minor irregularities** and negotiate with the most
  advantageous respondent(s).
- Recommendation to the **HLB Board**, which has sole discretion.
- Selected partner enters **Exclusive Negotiation Period**.
- Confidential until award; submissions become HLB property/public after award
  except properly marked proprietary information.
